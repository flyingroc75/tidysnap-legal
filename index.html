📷 TidySnap

TidySnap is an invitation-only tidying coach for a few households. Prop a phone somewhere with a clear view of the room, start a session, and it takes photos on a timer, reviews them, and speaks one tidy task at a time — finishing with a short before-and-after summary of what got better.

Access is by invitation only: sign-in is with Google, limited to email addresses the operator has invited.
