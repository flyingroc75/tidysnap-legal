TidySnap Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

TidySnap is a small, invitation-only household tidying coach operated by an individual ("we", "us") for a handful of invited households. This policy describes what information the service handles and why. There are no ads, no analytics trackers, and no sale of personal information.

Who can use TidySnap

Access is limited to email addresses the operator has explicitly invited. Signing in uses Google Sign-In; if your email address is not on the invitation list, you cannot use the service and no household is created for you.

Information we collect

  • Google account identity. When you sign in, Google tells us your name and email address (the basic profile and email scopes only). We use the email address solely to recognize you and place you in your household. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, photos, or any other Google data.
  • Session photos. During a tidying session you start, devices you sign in with take periodic photos of the rooms you point them at. These photos are the core input of the service.
  • House notes. Free-text notes you write about your home (for example, items that are fine where they are).
  • Session records. The tidying suggestions generated during your sessions, before-and-after summary photos, and short written summaries of what improved.

How your photos are processed

Session photos are sent to the Anthropic API (Claude) to generate tidying suggestions and session summaries. Under Anthropic's API terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train their models. Photos are processed for no other purpose and are not shared with any other third party.

Where data is stored, and for how long

  • Everything is stored on the operator's private server — not on a commercial cloud hosting account.
  • Session photos are automatically deleted within about 24 hours, except the before-and-after pair kept with each session's summary.
  • Session suggestions, summaries, and house notes are kept until you ask us to delete them, so the service can remember your home across sessions.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • We never use your photos or notes for advertising.
  • We never share your information with third parties other than the AI processing described above.

Your choices

You can stop using TidySnap at any time simply by not signing in. To have your household's stored data (photos, notes, summaries, and records) deleted, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

Children

TidySnap is intended for adults managing their own households and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
